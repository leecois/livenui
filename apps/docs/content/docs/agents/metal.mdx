## Interactions

The ring preview supports a circular action and a pill button. Move the pointer around its edge
to deform the metal and see its reflection on the adjacent label in dark mode.
The action copies the current configuration.

Text uses a compact glyph mask with a neighboring text reflection. The badge uses the short
label `New` to fit its fixed pill geometry. All three components support Pause and reduced motion,
and each gallery preset keeps its own material without changing neighboring samples.

Keep `normalizeHostStyles={false}` when the child uses your accent gradient. The shader sits
above the host content and preserves its fill. Use `useMetalBend` on the component reference
to enable pointer deformation in your application.
