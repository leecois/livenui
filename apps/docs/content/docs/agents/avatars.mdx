## Material and motion

The default preview uses Fabric at 96px with the renderer's fur and lighting presets.
Choose Plastic for a glossy surface, or explore the other materials in Studio.
Move your pointer around the preview to change the bot's gaze, click it to hop, and use
Idle, Working or Sleeping to compare its motion. Pause freezes playback.
